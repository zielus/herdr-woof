# Woof logo concepts

Concept exploration using the built-in image generation tool. Reference: ../herdr-logo.jpg. These are raster concepts, not final vector artwork.

## Shared prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Generate one original logo concept for woof, a durable graph workflow engine built on Herdr, named after a herding dog's bark. Reference image is style inspiration only: Herdr's lavender sheep on dark charcoal, bold cutout silhouette and terminal wit. Create a DIFFERENT companion logo, not a redraw of the sheep. Flat vector-like raster artwork, lavender #c3c7f5 on solid charcoal #1c1c28. One centered large symbol with generous margins, square small concept preview. No gradients, shadows, mockups, texture, outlines around canvas, labels, captions, wordmarks or watermark.

## 01-barking-collie

A striking side-profile border collie head facing left, open muzzle barking, two tiny angular bark strokes. A clever small terminal chevron eye subtly echoes the sheep reference. Bold silhouette, pointed ear and distinctive collie neck ruff.

## 02-speech-bubble

An exceptionally simple rounded speech bubble whose silhouette is also a border collie head: pointed ears on top, a short muzzle forming the bubble tail, negative-space eye and blaze. Convey a dog's woof as communication. Clever polished minimal symbol.

## 03-dog-and-sheep

A sophisticated figure-ground mark: a large border collie profile in lavender, with a small sheep profile revealed in the charcoal negative space of its neck. Two readable animals in one compact silhouette, communicating shepherding. Keep very simple.

## 04-workflow-trail

A minimalist running herding dog built from a single thick flowing lavender path, with exactly three circular node accents integrated along the path, subtly suggesting a directed workflow. Readable dog first, workflow second. Dynamic horizontal silhouette centered in square.

## 05-w-monogram

A bold geometric W monogram that also reads as a forward-facing border collie: outer tips form pointed ears, the central negative space forms a long white facial blaze and muzzle. Symmetric, exceptionally compact, memorable at favicon size. No other lettering.

## 06-terminal-bark

A minimal collie profile formed from a chunky terminal greater-than chevron for the muzzle and a rounded ear/head contour, with a short underscore-like bark dash in front. Integrate terminal syntax into a charming dog, not a terminal window. As simple and iconic as the reference.

## 07-herding-orbit

A compact circular symbol: a sleek herding dog curls around three simple sheep-like rounded dots, the dog's body and tail create an almost closed protective orbit. A visual metaphor for guiding a workflow. Large clear masses, extremely few details.

## 08-woolly-mascot

A charming original front-facing shaggy sheepdog head with floppy ears, a broad woolly rounded silhouette, a small charcoal nose and tiny terminal-chevron eyes. Echo the reference sheep's soft shape while clearly being a friendly working dog. Graphic silhouette, restrained personality, no detailed fur.
